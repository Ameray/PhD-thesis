>> pools from CBM
>>1= Very fast aboveground=Live stemwood of merchantable size+L horizon
>>2= Very fast belowground=Live branches, stumps and small trees+Dead fine roots in the mineral soil (diameter<5mm)
>>3= Fast aboveground=Leaves+Fine and small woody debris plus dead coarse roots in the forest floor, approximately ≥5 and <75mm diameter
>>4= Fast belowground=Dead coarse roots in the mineral soil approximately ≥5 diameter
>>5= Medium=Fine roots+Coarse woody debris on the ground. It may be on the surface or buried within the forest floor+
>>6= Slow aboveground=Coarse roots+ F H and O horizons
>>7=Slow belowground=Humified organic matter in the mineral soil
>>8= Snag stem=Dead standing stemwood of merchantable size
>>9= Snag other=Dead branches, stumps and small trees.
>>10= Extra= decaying pool written into the code but with no inputs at this time
